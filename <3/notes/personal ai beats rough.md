π: I’d give the essay **seven core beats**, in this order:

1. **General AI does my chores. Personal AI helps me scale myself.**  
    Open with the difference in purpose: handling things that don’t need your uniqueness versus extending the things that do.
    
2. **What is the “self” I’m trying to scale?**  
    Introduce your working model: observe, understand, value, react, act. Your particular way of doing these—your taste, judgment, intuition—is what you want personal AI to understand.
    
3. **What would scaling that actually mean?**  
    Apply something approaching your own functions across more places and longer durations than you could individually. Your attention and capacity are limited; the world contains far more than you can encounter.
    
4. **Three ways this becomes useful.**  
    **Scout:** find people, ideas, and expressions you would value. **Filter:** guard your attention and shape what reaches you. **Manifest:** help feelings become ideas, and ideas become things that reflect your taste.
    
5. **These are expressions of one understanding of me.**  
    What you notice, what you value, and what you create are interconnected. Introduce the permeable aura: personal AI mediating between your internal world and the external world, increasingly molded to your shape.
    
6. **It learns who I am by encountering me in motion.**  
    Instructions only capture part of you. Choices, reactions, conversations, and revisions reveal things you may struggle to articulate. Introduce Ghost here as your starting point for collecting those meaningful expressions.
    
7. **The larger possibility is ownership of my experience of life.**  
    Return to who decides what reaches you and what receives your attention. Personal AI is a way to bring more of that direction into your own hands. Close with this as an exploration you’re beginning to build.
    

The emotional thread running through all seven is **uniqueness → expression → freedom**. I’d keep browser mechanics, dashboards, connection counts, and personal stock for the other artifacts. The feeling → idea distinction deserves space here because it makes “scale myself” especially tangible.
