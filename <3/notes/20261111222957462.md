**beliefs**

as rate of change accelerates, it becomes much more difficult to make sense of how the world is changing. therefore, it will become increasingly tempting to avoid any and all efforts to make sense of the broader changes taking place and focus all of our attention on experiments in the present moment that are more legible and clear.

yet, the faster our world changes, and the more difficult it becomes to make sense of the future, the more valuable it becomes to do so.

for example, almost all of the ideas we are working on today arent going to matter within a decade given the broader changes taking place – such as the emergence of superintelligence.

(like ants building a molehill on a fault line right before an earthquake, most of us are working on ideas, and in ways, that will not create any value for humanity across this upcoming decade).

when we approach ideas as we always have, with a sorely outdated understanding of the truths of our world which have shifted rapidly under our feet, we will be far more likely to fail despite seeming like we got off to a great start because other similarly deluded minds cheer us on to walk off of cliffs.

the following are my beliefs about our world and how its changing that i hold to be true. ive done my best to present my beliefs in the sharpest way possible, to encourage, and make it easier for, sharp criticism.

and this document and every single change i make to it moving forward is backed onchain. i want irrefutable evidence of the quality of my judgement and how my understanding evolves over time.

---

- ai will never surpass human potential.
- it will become exponentially more difficult to create value.
- someone great at one skill, will increasingly be great at all skills.
- the power law will eat the world.
- ai will eat all work that does not have space for individual uniqueness.
- the market will be increasingly deluded about which people and ideas to back.
- the more powerful ai becomes, the more decentralized our world will become.
