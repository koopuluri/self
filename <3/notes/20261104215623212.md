really if i were to sum up the entire process for my creation / seeking / exploration or whatever the fuck this is that i'm doing, it would be: letting go and moving into subtler and subtler truths within myself.

like i can't explain in words the importance of cultivating an incredibly present, grounded, mindful, aware, balanced mind.

that is the source of anything worthwhile.

an agitated mind only produces things that will agitate the world.

it is your responsibility - not just for your own wellbeing, but also for those who may consume what you put out into the world - to create from a sense of presence and balance.

and honestly, there is no better way to live life.

i want to deepen my practice, i want to deepen my awareness, i want to deepen my balance. i want to be fully present. i want to wield my attention the way musashi wielded his swords. masterfully.
