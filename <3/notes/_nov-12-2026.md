a really powerful short film esque scene that i want to make one day. it's an opening shot to the film / video or whatever. i'm not even sure what the entire artifact is going to be, but i can see the opening crystal clearly:

i'm in a white room. white on all sides. sterile. a hospital setting.

the shot opens with me in the center of the frame. there is a wooden chair right behind me, so the audience can't see it yet, but i'm standing right in front of it.

i look around to the left and right with a look of panic. 5 white men in suits (agent smith esque) walk briskly at me from all sides, push me back onto the chair and pin me to the chair as i fight back intensely trying to break free.

but i can't.

camera: as they are pushing me back toward the chair, the camera is tracking. after i've sat in the chair, the camera zooms into my eyes. linear zoom, not on a curve, just slowly getting to my face, and once my face is centered, it starts slantedly upward toward my eyes - so that my eyes are centered. i am furious and afraid.

one of the agents stabs my third eye region with some kind of syringe. i stop resisting after that lost in some kind of trance - as if my soul is leaving my body. the camera keeps zooming in and stops once it's literally my eyes in the frame. like so zoomed in that the entire frame is basically my eyes. after the syringe injected me, my eyes turned white.

after stopping briefly at my white eyes for a moment (during which the music stops and everything goes silent), the camera starts zooming out. and as it is zooming out it shows me in the same suit the agents are wearing, sitting in an open office floor plan with many other folks with suits working behind me, as i sit there still looking straight at the audience with my white eyes, a dead expression, as i am typing away on my laptop on the desk right in front of me.

the camera basically reverses the same movement symmetrically. zoom out and then track backward to show the office.
