# the depth of human potential

the old world is gone. the playbooks have become useless. i want to explore what it means to unlock human potential in the age of superintelligence.

i believe that AI will never exceed the depth of human capability. but that belief asks something of us: we will have to access depths of our potential that we have never had to reach before. this will require a new mindset, new approaches, new tools and instruments. i want to find out what they are—to uncover the stack of knowledge and ways of working that can help us get there.

right now, i see three parts to this: personhood, personal intelligence, and personal stock. identity, leverage, and incentive.

personhood starts with reconsidering what it means to be human. we have been conditioned by centuries of doing work that is fundamentally universal and impersonal. much of our sense of ourselves has formed around that work. it is also precisely the kind of work that superintelligence will do far better than us.

we need to lean into the aspects of the human condition that we are uniquely suited to explore. if we want to use superintelligence as a tool, we have to develop a deeper understanding of who we are and what we want to do with it. the stakes are personal: whether we direct these tools or allow them to direct us.

personal intelligence gives that identity leverage. general intelligence is a commodity. personal intelligence is the amplification of our unique shape.

people underestimate the depth of individual uniqueness. i believe an individual's uniqueness is as deep as the general intelligence we share. there is that much to discover, understand, and amplify in a single person. an intelligence built around that depth could amplify everything they do.

> within a decade, i can imagine someone raising a trillion dollars on their personal stock to train their personal intelligence. that sounds extraordinary only if we underestimate how much there is in an individual to develop.

personal stock is the third part: incentive. accessing the depths of our potential will be more difficult than anything we have ever done. we will need as much support and as many resources as we can get. personal stock could give others an incentive to provide that support—to invest in the person and the journey of realizing their potential.

these three ideas belong together. personhood helps us understand our unique shape. personal intelligence helps us amplify it. personal stock helps us gather the resources and support to develop it.

this is what i want to explore: how we reach the depths of the human potential we all possess, with the tools now becoming available to us.

> have faith. trust in yourself.
