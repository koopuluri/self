on streaming:

  

when i say streaming im not talking about live streaming but rather a way of operating, perhaps you could even call it a "workflow". 

  

when i say streaming, im referring to streaming your consciousness. thoughts arise in the mind and you translate those thoughts into text with no gap between the thought and the expression of that thought into text.

  

ok but practically speaking, there will be a gap. but ive found by practice that you can shave that gap down to be really small, and eventually dissolve it altogether. when the gap is dissolved, you become a witness to the thoughts emerging in your mind and flowing out of you onto the screen in front of you – as one continuous flow, without you impeding it in any way.

  

of course there is certainly some subconscious pruning that is going on. your subconscious is probably figuring out what trains of thought to allow and which to hide - because it feels vulnerable to see your thinking in words somehow, and your mind probably prevents you from seeing some of your deepest hidden trains of thought that it may not want to admit? im not sure how or what happens under the hood, but im sure theres something going on that is pruning / determining what trains to allow to be streamed.

  

ok but thats kind of besides the point. what is the point im trying to get at? ok what is streaming and why is it useful? thats the crux of what im trying to explore rn.

  

ok so streaming is simple: its basically a stream of consciousness: expressing your thoughts as they arise in the mind. if it is a visual, you can draw it on a piece of paper and drop the pic of it in the stream. you can describe it in words, whatever. the point is to get out of the way of your mind and allow it to move naturally. thats the point. there is no forcing. "you" do not determine what you think about. you do not force a direction that your mind is not feeling like exploring in the moment. you literally allow whatever emerges to express itself.

  

the more you do it, the more easily you can dissociate between you and your thoughts. it becomes very clear that your thoughts emerge on their own due to your conditioning, and that you dont really actively "think". shows just how clunky our language is to desribe the process of thinking. it isnt an active process that you sort of put effort and do. it happens.

  

so does that mean we have no control? ofc not. we certainly do. and our control is restricted to our attention. you can choose to let go of a thought and not "build" on it to form a train of thoughts, and wait for the next one.

  

you have the choice for which thoughts you pay attention to and build on, and which ones you let go of.

  

ok that language was too strong. i think its more accurate to say that you can have such control over attention, but it is a skill to be worked on, and does not come naturally to most (if any) of us.

  

what you pay attention to, gains energy. if you have a negative thought, and fail to let go of it and hold onto it with your attention and allow it to form a train - you can quickly enter into a negative spiral. and ofc the same with positive. actually maybe not, it seems positive cycles are more difficult? ok i have to clarify what positive means here. will deal with this nuance later. basically my point is that peace is not found in these reactive trains of thought trains / spirals, peace is found in letting them go, so its imp to distinguish between negative, positive and peace. like positive trains can lead to mania, and negative trains can lead to anxiety, and peace is a diff dimension altogether.

  

ok where was i? alright im talking about streaming. streaming is the process of translating your thinking into an external form (text drawings etc) as they emerge with little to no "gap" in which you process it before you express it. that is what i mean by streaming.

  

why? why do i value streaming? what is so valuable about this process? why now? there is something about this new state of our world that enables and rewards streaming in ways that didnt make sense before. lets explore all of this.

  

and maybe before that let me talk about some of the "best practices" ive found. well they prob arent anywhere close to best, but whatever, they feel like improvements to how i used to this before.

  

firstly, a stream goes in one direction. there is no going back and tweaking something youve expressed. now, you can def go back and redact something youve said, bec maybe its private and you dont want it in an external form. that may be valuable, or at least knowing you can do that lets you ease up a bit, but ok besides redacting you cant go back and start shaping something within the stream in the stream itself.

  

for ex: you cant go up to say a part of the stream that you think would be something good to post on twitter or turn into an essay and start editing it right there in the stream. that is not allowed. if you want to "shape" an expression into an artifact that you publish or whatever, you copy it from the stream and paste it elsewhere and craft it.

  

the stream is meant to be a one way flow forward. like time. you cant go backward.
