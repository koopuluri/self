## Design

1. **Natural**. It must feel obvious. A person experiencing it should feel that they would have come up with the same thing if they tried. Simple. Not special. Easy.
2. **Fundamental**. It must strive toward the essence, the root, the source. Something from which many things can grow from.
3. **Beautiful**. It must pull. Beauty pulls. Beautiful typography pulls you to read. Beautiful pen pulls you to write. Beautiful home pulls you to live. Beauty is beautiful.

## AI

1. **AI must never speak as me.** Any dm, reply, post, email, etc. that comes from me (my username / email / etc.) must never be drafted by AI.
