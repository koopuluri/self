---
title: The power law will eat the world
date: November 2026
---
When we express creatively, and can distribute our creations broadly and quickly, we will find outcomes concentrate extremely toward the best – i.e., a power law distribution.

The power law distribution reflects human nature. We want the best. And it happens to be the case that we broadly agree on what the best options are; that there is an objective sense of quality.

We already see concentrated outcomes in creative domains in which broad, fast distribution exists. Software, tech, art, content etc. Exhibit an extreme power law distribution of outcomes – the best capture the majority of revenue leaving little for the rest. And the median outcome is failure.

But the vast majority of domains / work are still normally distributed: outcomes are spread across a much larger pool of participants. This has been the norm for much of the past 50 years or so.

One of my core bets about the future is that concentrated outcomes will manifest across _every_ economically valuable activity, not just a few as is the case today, and will be far more extreme (further concentrated toward a smaller percentage of participants) than we have ever seen.

To see why, we have to understand what leads to concentrated outcomes.

I see broadly 4 factors that lead to concentrated outcomes for a kind of work:

1. **Complexity**: the more ways there are to do something (the more creative the task is), the greater the skill gap between the best and the rest, and thus the more extremely outcomes concentrate toward the best.
2. **Distribution**: the easier and faster it is to distribute your creation broadly, the more extremely outcomes concentrate toward the best, because more people can choose the best.
3. **Competition**: the more participants compete, and the better they are, the more extremely outcomes concentrate toward the best. Because better competition pushes everyone to be better and unlocks objectively better products that have more universal appeal that more people choose, leading to more concentrated outcomes.
4. **Leverage:** the faster you can translate ideas to reality, the faster the best break away from the rest – a force multiplier.

Progress in AI, and broadly progress itself, amplifies all 4 of these factors.

By doing the less creative work for us, AI pushes us toward more creative work that will lead to a far greater difference in skill between the best and the rest.

Embodied AI will unlock broad and fast distribution for many kinds of products that dont have it today. for example, when the best pasta chef can "cook" for billions of people _through_ their personal robots, why would anyone eat pasta from anyone but the top few pasta chefs in the world?

Similarly, AI will help individuals scale their judgement. When the best financial advisor will be able to serve millions of clients rather than just dozens, many more will be able work with the best.

As our world progresses politically and it becomes easier to distribute our ideas and products more widely, more seamlessly, outcomes will further concentrate toward the best.

As more people gain access to quality education and learn how to create, it will increase quality of competition, further concentrating outcomes toward the best who are now much better due to competition.
