> Here’s how I’d use the terms:

> So rather than asking **“where do we cut?”**, I’d ask **“what does this bubble need?”** I’d route it like this:
> 
> 1. **Handle the obvious cases locally.** A direct reply or beat link brings that beat into context. A quick “yep, do that” right after a proposal continues the exchange. No extra model call.
