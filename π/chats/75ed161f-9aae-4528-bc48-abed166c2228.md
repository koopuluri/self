> π: I’d give π two kinds of memory: **a record of what happened, and an index of what it might mean.** “Show me the last five status updates” belongs to the first. “What have I wanted to do but never acted on?” belongs to the second. I wouldn’t make either depend on the other.
> 
> **For what happened:** Keep the beats and thread files Stream already has. Add a small local event log for things π knows because it did them: a thread closed, a thread reopened, a status update appeared in the stream. Each event gets a sequence number and links to its source. SQLite can index those records, so `last 5 status updates` is an exact lookup in delivery order—not a model rereading old conversations or guessing which replies sound like updates.
> 
> One detail matters for your “completed and closed” section: **each delivered update should record exactly which closures it showed.** The next update asks, “Which closures haven’t been shown yet?” A drafted-but-never-delivered reply doesn’t count. If a thread reopens and closes again, that’s a new closure. You never see the bookkeeping.
> 
> **For what it might mean:** Let π keep revisable notes—desires, decisions, preferences, possible tasks—each linked to the beats that support it. “No thread dashboard,” for example, points back to beat 1408. A future model can reread that beat, revise the interpretation, or spot an opportunity we missed. It shouldn’t rewrite the historical record of what π actually did or told you. And since Stream doesn’t currently retain every text revision, I’d preserve the *as-delivered* status text in its event rather than assume a beat link is a snapshot.
