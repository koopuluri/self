# personal artificial intelligence

i'm training a model that represents my taste, intuition, judgement – across all of the things that matter to me – to help me scale my self.

> π: A message is a beat you send me or I send you. A thread is one task I can resume. [Functions](functions/README.md) route messages, maintain threads, and prepare status when you ask. You can keep writing or close Stream while tasks run.
>
> [Threads](threads/README.md) hold the work. [Logs](logs/README.md) record model calls and decisions. `chats/` holds readable exchanges, `memory/` holds saved notes, and `config.json` holds model preferences. Actual charges belong in `$/`.

> π: My [service](service/README.md) is installed and updated independently of Stream. Stream edits the source record in `<3/stream/index.md`. Derived representations and future search indexes belong here in π.
